About Pacific Trails Resort

Welcome to Pacific Trails Resort, a peaceful coastal getaway where guests can relax, explore nature, and enjoy the beauty of California's North Coast. Our resort offers a comfortable escape from busy everyday life, surrounded by breathtaking ocean views, fresh coastal air, and beautiful natural scenery.

At Pacific Trails Resort, we believe a memorable vacation should combine relaxation with adventure. Guests can stay in comfortable yurts, explore scenic hiking trails, enjoy guided nature walks, and spend time discovering the nearby redwood forests. Whether you prefer a quiet morning overlooking the Pacific Ocean or an afternoon exploring the outdoors, there is something for everyone.

Our goal is to provide a welcoming environment where families, couples, and individual travelers can create lasting memories. We invite you to experience the natural beauty, peaceful atmosphere, and outdoor adventures that make Pacific Trails Resort a special destination.

Experience the Beauty of Pacific Trails

Discover the natural beauty of the California coast. From peaceful ocean views to scenic hiking trails, Pacific Trails Resort offers outdoor adventures and relaxing experiences for every guest.
